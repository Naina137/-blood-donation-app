# LifePulse - Verified Real-Time Blood Donation & Emergency SOS Platform 🩸

A genuine, production-grade, clinical-standard web application designed to connect patients in urgent medical emergencies directly with verified voluntary blood donors, licensed blood banks, and community donation drives.

---

## 🌟 Key Genuine & Realistic Features

### 1. 🚨 Live Hospital Emergency SOS Feed
- **Real-Time Urgency Classification**:
  - `CRITICAL_IMMEDIATE`: Life-threatening conditions (trauma, active hemorrhages, ICU) requiring blood within <2 hours.
  - `HIGH_24H`: Urgent surgical cases, pediatric cardiac surgeries, Thalassemia major transfusions.
  - `STANDARD_PLANNED`: Scheduled cancer chemotherapy platelet transfusions and elective surgeries.
- **100% Medical Record Verification**: Displays hospital name, ward/bed number, attending surgeon/physician name, and IPD case numbers.
- **Fulfillment Tracker**: Visual progress bar indicating needed units vs pledged units.
- **One-Click WhatsApp SOS Alert Generator**: Generates formatted, authentic emergency broadcast messages with direct phone dialers.
- **Direct Attendant Call**: Instant dialer connection to the on-duty family attendant.

### 2. 🔍 Verified Voluntary Donor Registry & Search
- Filter donors by **Blood Group (A+, A-, B+, B-, AB+, AB-, O+, O-)**, **City/Metropolitan Region**, and **Real-Time Availability**.
- **Verified Credentials**: Government ID verification badges and Hospital Donation records.
- **Donor Tier Levels**: Bronze Supporter, Silver LifeSaver, Gold Guardian, and Platinum Champion.
- **Privacy Protection Mode**: Option for donors to mask their mobile numbers (`+91 98*** **419`) to eliminate telemarketing/spam, routing requests via automated SMS/push prompts.

### 3. 🩺 WHO-Compliant Medical Eligibility Pre-Screening
Before registering or pledging blood, prospective donors undergo an interactive 8-point medical safety test:
- Age validation (18 - 65 years)
- Minimum weight threshold (≥ 50 kg / 110 lbs)
- Hemoglobin screening (≥ 12.5 g/dL)
- Deferral criteria: No tattoos/piercings within 6 months, no major surgeries within 6 months, no infections/antibiotics within 14 days, alcohol-free for 24 hours, and adherence to safe donation intervals (90 days for men, 120 days for women).

### 4. 🪪 Digital Verified Blood Donor Card
- Generates an official, high-resolution Digital Donor Card.
- Complete with unique Donor ID (`LP-DNR-XXXX`), blood group badge, verification seal, issue date, and simulated QR verification code.
- One-click **Print / Save as PDF** support.

### 5. 🏥 Real-Time Blood Bank Inventory & Stock Tracker
- Monitors stock availability across Government Medical Centers, Red Cross Societies, and Hospital-attached blood banks.
- Color-coded stock level badges: Critical Shortage (≤3 units), Low Stock (4-10 units), and Adequate (>10 units).
- Highlights apheresis Single Donor Platelet (SDP) separators, cryoprecipitate availability, and 24x7 emergency blood transport ambulances.

### 6. 📅 Voluntary Donation Camps & Drive Pass Booking
- Explore upcoming community, collegiate, and corporate donation drives organized by Red Cross and Rotary.
- Reserve a priority donation time-slot and receive a digital camp entry pass with token ID.

### 7. 🧬 Interactive Blood Compatibility Matrix
- Visual matching matrix: select any of the 8 blood types to immediately see who you can safely donate red blood cells to and receive from.
- Educational guide clarifying Universal Donor (O-) and Universal Recipient (AB+).
- The 4 stages of donation and scientific myth-busting guide.

### 2. Start the Development Server:
```bash

```
